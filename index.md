## Account & Data Deletion Request
In accordance with Apple App Store and Google Play Store policies, you have the right to request the deletion of your user account and all associated data.

**Note:** SkillMatchIQ primarily uses anonymous device identifiers. If you have not linked an email address or purchased a subscription, simply uninstalling the app will remove local data.

### How to Request Deletion
To request permanent deletion of your data from our servers, please click the link below.

👉 **[Request Account Deletion via Email](mailto:support@skillmatchiq.com?subject=Account%20Deletion%20Request%20-%20SkillMatchIQ&body=I%20am%20requesting%20the%20deletion%20of%20my%20SkillMatchIQ%20account%20and%20data.%0A%0AUser%20ID%20(Optional%20-%20found%20in%20Settings):%20%0A%0AAssociated%20Email%20(if%20applicable):%20)**

*This link opens your default email client. Please allow up to 30 days for processing.*

### Data That Will Be Deleted
Upon processing your request, the following data will be permanently removed:
*   **Profile Data:** Your industry preferences, experience level, and tone settings.
*   **Uploaded Content:** Any resume text or job descriptions processed by our servers.
*   **Analysis History:** Your match scores, skill gap analysis results, and generated insights.
*   **Learning Timelines:** Your saved progress, milestones, and selected learning paths.
*   **Identifiers:** Your anonymous user ID and any associated RevenueCat subscription tokens on our backend.

### Important Note on Subscriptions
**Deleting your account does NOT automatically cancel your subscription.**
Since subscriptions are managed directly by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store), you must cancel them manually to prevent future charges.

*   **iOS:** Go to Settings -> Apple ID -> Subscriptions -> SkillMatchIQ.
*   **Android:** Open Google Play -> Profile Icon -> Payments & subscriptions.
